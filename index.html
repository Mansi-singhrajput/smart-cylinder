LPG Safety & Precaution Guidelines

Follow standard safety norms to keep your family and home protected.

👃

Gas Leakage Precautions

If you detect the smell of LPG (Ethyl Mercaptan), do not turn electrical switches on or off. Extinguish all open flames and open windows immediately.

🛡️

Check Safety Seal & Weight

Ensure the white plastic safety cap and seal are intact upon delivery. Check the tare weight stamped on cylinder collar ring.

🔝

Always Keep Upright

Store cylinders vertically on a flat, firm surface at ground level. Never keep cylinders horizontally or in confined basements.

🚨

Emergency Helpline: 1906

Dial national 24-hour toll-free emergency helpline 1906 for immediate dispatch of emergency leak technicians.
